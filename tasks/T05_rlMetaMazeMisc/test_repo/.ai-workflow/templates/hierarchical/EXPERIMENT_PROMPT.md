# Experiment execution brief

## Outcome
Produce decision-relevant empirical evidence for the DIC while preserving the frozen protocol.

## Method boundary
Use controlled conditions, objective graders, seeds/statistics appropriate to the claim, complete logs, and explicit separation of smoke, pilot, confirmatory, and post-hoc evidence. Do not silently change hypotheses or evaluation gates.

## Deliverable
Runnable code/configuration, raw and summarized evidence, failure analysis, claim-ledger updates, and Action→Prompt/Run→EPS→DIC lineage. Escalate material protocol or claim changes; otherwise proceed autonomously.
