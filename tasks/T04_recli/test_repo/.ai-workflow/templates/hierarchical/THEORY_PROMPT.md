# Theory execution brief

## Outcome
Resolve the DIC's mathematical, conceptual, literature, or novelty question as strongly as evidence permits.

## Method boundary
Search primary sources when needed; state domains and assumptions; attempt counterexamples; distinguish definition, construction invariant, theorem, empirical hypothesis, conjecture, and unsupported claim. Do not convert plausible intuition into a theorem.

## Deliverable
Proofs or counterexamples, source-backed novelty analysis, claim-ledger updates, exact unresolved points, and Action→Prompt/Run→EPS→DIC lineage. Escalate material claim or objective changes; otherwise proceed autonomously.
