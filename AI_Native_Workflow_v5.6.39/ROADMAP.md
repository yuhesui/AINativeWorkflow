# AI Native Workflow — Roadmap

This file contains **future work only**. Current truth belongs in `CURRENT_STATE.md`.

## Active Programme Objective

Empirically test whether the current hierarchy and verification/recovery mechanism increases trustworthy long-horizon progress while reducing or reallocating human supervisory attention relative to matched alternatives.

## Immediate Phase Candidates

### 1. Matched-Budget Evaluation

Execute the frozen comparison across direct/reactive, flat plan-and-execute, hierarchy-only, and hierarchy-plus-verification/recovery conditions.

Required additions:

- frozen model/surface profiles;
- objective graders;
- effective-horizon factors;
- injected-fault protocol;
- human-intervention taxonomy;
- manual routing/context-transfer counts;
- attention duration logging where feasible;
- verified-progress milestone weights frozen before outcome inspection.

### 2. Human-Attention Measurement Pilot

Test whether `rho_H` and its raw components are practical to measure without the logging procedure dominating the task. Compare total attention and the proportion spent on routine coordination versus scientific judgement.

### 3. DIC Comprehension / Human Interface Study

Measure whether researchers can recover Primary Aim, current evidence, unresolved questions, and affected dependency scope faster from the multi-view DIC/handoff than from raw agent histories or flat status reports.

### 4. Dependency Declaration Robustness

Stress hidden/incorrect dependency edges and quantify when local recovery becomes unsafe. Explore assisted dependency discovery while retaining explicit uncertainty.

## Publication Programme

### Meta-Agents

Keep the Full Paper as the primary format. Insert only verified new results; do not expand to 9 pages merely because the limit allows it. Preserve the systems/theory contribution and responsible-use boundary.

### NeurIPS Education

Develop 15–20 minutes of teaching material so it can be cut to a shorter slot if required. Focus on a reusable research workflow researchers can adopt or adapt, starting from existing informal ChatGPT/Research/Codex workflows and the problem of human supervisory attention.

### Who Verifies

Keep the current Demo Paper frozen unless a correction or submission-format issue requires an update.
