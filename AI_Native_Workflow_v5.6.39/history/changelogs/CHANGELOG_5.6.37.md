# Changelog - v5.6.37

## Scope

Minor conceptual consolidation after the v5.6.36 Main/Orchestrator/prompting update. No scientific programme was reopened and no comparative performance result was invented.

## Human-attention objective

- Replaced loose time-saving language with the more precise design target: **human attention required per unit of verified research progress**.
- Formalized human-attention intensity in the technical report as a benchmark-relative evaluation quantity, not an established efficiency result.
- Clarified that routine coordination/state reconstruction/prompt routing/evidence bookkeeping/local repair should move below the human boundary, while objective choice, surprising evidence, material methodology changes, claim boundaries, and external responsibility remain human-attention priorities.

## DIC and research salience

- DIC now explicitly serves as both a durable machine contract and a **human-intelligibility projection** of complex execution.
- `DIC/README.md` foregrounds one Primary Aim and a primary/supporting/optional salience structure.
- Added guidance against **constraint-objective inversion / defensive drift**, where caution becomes the effective objective, and **salience flattening**, where many plausible branches dilute the central scientific contribution.
- These are documented as workflow failure modes/observations, not universal empirical properties of language models.

## Runtime guides

- Updated Main, Orchestrator, Research, Theory, Experiment, prompting-core, and checklist guidance around attention and salience.
- Preserved the current operational boundary: Main is persistent chat; Orchestrator is a repository-aware coding-agent execution surface.
- Preserved one DIC per Phase and DIC/PLAN as the durable execution graph.

## Technical report

- Strengthened the motivation around informal multi-surface research workflows already used by researchers.
- Added human-attention intensity and related experimental measurement design.
- Updated failure taxonomy and DIC-as-human-interface discussion.
- Synchronized current runtime/reference implementation semantics.

## Managing Agents that Manage Agents

- Selected the **Full Paper** format.
- Updated the paper to frame the contribution as a meta-agent management protocol rather than novelty of hierarchical planning itself.
- Added Primary Aim/salience and human-attention-intensity framing, with explicit non-claim of confirmed attention-efficiency gains.
- Preserved double-blind formatting and responsible-use statement.

## NeurIPS Education preparation

- Reframed the resource around **AI-Native Workflow for Research**: a formalized, adaptable version of the informal ChatGPT/research-agent/coding-agent workflows researchers already use.
- Hierarchical planning is taught as an enabling mechanism rather than the complete subject.
- Human attention and DIC intelligibility are central pedagogical themes.
