# AutoMLR eligibility and registration check

## Decision

**NOT SUBMISSION-READY — qualifying-result gate is not met.**

The current primary contribution is a hierarchical agent workflow/control system, its formal specification, and a planned evaluation. This reconstruction did **not** produce a separate qualifying machine-learning research result that an autonomous research agent conducted end-to-end or to which it made the decisive primary contribution. A systems-only workflow paper therefore should not be represented as eligible.

This conclusion is sufficient to stop deadline-critical effort even if abstract registration remains open. The official-page snapshot and automatically extracted deadline/registration text are stored in:

- `research/official_sources/automlr.html`;
- `research/OFFICIAL_WORKSHOP_REQUIREMENTS.md`;
- `research/WORKSHOP_REQUIREMENTS.json`.

The registration status must be re-opened on the official site before any future submission; no registration or external action was performed in this run.

## What would change the decision

Eligibility would require a distinct primary ML result—such as a completed, frozen, statistically defensible study of an ML method or discovery—where the autonomous system performed the qualifying research process end-to-end or made a demonstrably decisive contribution. Runtime unit tests, a dependency simulation, manuscript drafting, and planned experiments do not satisfy that bar.

## Scaffold policy

`main.tex` is retained as a **clearly watermarked scaffold only**. Part I contains no invented result. Part II records verified system facts and visibly marks text requiring human authorship/rewrite because the venue expects that part to be human-written. Part III is a reflection scaffold. It must not be uploaded until eligibility, registration, human-authorship, anonymity, and evidence gates all pass.
