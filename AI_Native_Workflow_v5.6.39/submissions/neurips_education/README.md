# NeurIPS 2026 Education Track - Preparation Surface

**Status:** submission preparation only; no external submission performed.

## Proposed concept

**AI Native Workflow for Research: From Informal AI Assistance to Controlled Long-Horizon Research**

Most researchers already compose an informal workflow - for example a persistent ChatGPT/Claude planning chat, a research surface for literature, and Codex/Claude Code for repository execution. The resource teaches how to formalize, adapt, or selectively strengthen such a workflow so current AI systems can take on more coordination and execution without requiring proportionally more researcher attention.

Hierarchical planning, durable DIC/EPS state, specialized reasoning, coding-agent orchestration, parallel subagents, evidence gates, repair/replanning, and human authority are taught as mechanisms inside the workflow rather than as separate products or mandatory modes.

The practical target is **lower human attention required per unit of verified research progress**. This is presented as a design/evaluation objective, not a confirmed efficiency result.
