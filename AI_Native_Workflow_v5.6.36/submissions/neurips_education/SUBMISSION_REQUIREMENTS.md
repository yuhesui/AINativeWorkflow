# Official NeurIPS 2026 Education Track Requirements

**Source checked:** NeurIPS 2026 Call for Educational Resources, 1 September 2026.

## Purpose and audience

The track seeks accessible, reusable explanations of emerging AI concepts, with minimal jargon, for first-time attendees, young researchers, educators, lecturers/research groups, and non-specialists.

## Required submission

1. A PDF of **no more than two pages**, using the `education, final` template without the checklist, covering:
   - concept summary;
   - level and prerequisite knowledge;
   - learning objectives and outcomes;
   - at least three recent papers from leading venues or widely cited arXiv work in 2022–2026;
   - summary of the teaching materials.
2. Original teaching materials in a ZIP **under 200 MB**. The ZIP may link to permanent external resources for large items.

## Review and presentation

- single-blind: author names must be included;
- submission deadline: **4 September 2026, Anywhere on Earth**;
- notification: **28 September 2026**;
- accepted resources may be talks or posters, will be made downloadable, may enter the official track repository, and are eligible for a People's Choice Award;
- presentation is in person at the main conference site in Sydney; at least one author must register and attend.

## Fit note

The proposed concept should be framed as **verification as execution control**, not as an introduction to established agent basics, Transformers, or software testing. The materials must be newly produced for the track rather than recycled unchanged from the workflow presentation.
